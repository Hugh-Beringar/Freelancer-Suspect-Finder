THE FIELD GUIDE

Good intel. Better odds.

  1. Copy the briefing. Pick a hair type and set each listed look to Yes. Leave everything else on Any.
  2. Compare the faces. Open a portrait for a closer look. Extra looks are allowed: the briefing may describe only three of a suspect’s four traits.
  3. Work the field. Use No only for confirmed absent traits. Rule out a portrait when you’ve eliminated that suspect. Undo or show ruled-out profiles to restore them.
  4. Confirm the leader. Tells and meeting agendas vary by mission; they aren’t determined by the portraits.
Your filters and ruled-out profiles save in this browser when local storage is available. New showdown clears the board. Everything works offline; no account or server is needed.

About this archive

Based on Riximilian’s cheatsheet. The 72 portraits, reference labels, and trait records are sourced from flamewing’s community leader finder (Apache 2.0; bundled license). This app adds a new interface, absence filters, saved progress, and elimination tracking.

Reference names aren’t the randomized names shown in a mission. The data follows the cheatsheet, including its treatment of glasses resting on Bald Male 2’s hat as Hat only. This is a community reference, not live game data.
